## Method

- Data: `~/.config/stillroom/{ledger.json,prs.json,sessions.json,waits.json,projects.json}`, worktrees under `~/.config/stillroom/worktrees/video-client/`, transcripts under `~/.claude/projects/`, `gh pr view` for PR fate.
- Ledger rows deduplicated on (session, status, lines, next). Session-level status is the last row.
- Transcripts: counts across all 45; the 18 partly or row-less sessions read as condensed traces (full for files under 600 lines, last 300 lines plus a 45-entry window before each partly row for larger ones).
- Edits after the agent measured by `Co-Authored-By: Claude` trailers; a commit without one and not a merge counts as hers.
- Nothing scored about her speed, approvals or habits. The harness is the subject.
