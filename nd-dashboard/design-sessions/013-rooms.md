# 013 — Rooms: Rounds merged into Projects

**Asked:** "Merge Rounds and Projects. Rounds is overwhelming and forces me to switch context. Have the Rounds be per project on the project view itself; sidebar nav by priority; a place for items without a project. I want to spend an hour solving just Release 15.1 issues, then an hour on LetsO bugs."

**Roundtable:** interaction designer, flow and priority specialist, executive-function coach.

## Decided

- **The sidebar is the project list.** New session, then projects by her priority (High, Med, Low; inside a level, rooms with something for her first, then in flight), then No project, then "N off" behind one line, then Add a project. Older, Pull requests and Ledger sit at the bottom. Each row: name and one word: "2 need you", "3 in flight", or the blocked state's word when something is blocked. Never a count that outranks her priority.
- **A room per project.** Its head: name, priority, "2 need you, 3 in flight." Then the accordion of what needs her here (blocked first: permission, question, failed; then your turn; then PRs and waits that came back; inside a group her High/Med/Low, then Pinned order, then longest waiting). One open at a time; after Send the next opens with the cursor in its box. Then In flight and Waiting on others as one-line rows, New session in this project, Edit this project.
- **Rounds, Projects, Pinned and Needs you leave the sidebar.** The Rounds screen and the Projects screen are gone. A star means top of its room.
- **The good place.** When nothing in the room needs her: a plate that says "Nothing here needs you. 3 in flight." and one button, "Open LetsO bugs, 2 need you", pointing at the highest-priority room with something for her. Never a jump on its own.
- **Elsewhere.** Only a blocked item in another room shows, as one line at the foot: "Elsewhere: VDC-2330 is asking permission. Open it."
- **An hour, if she asks.** "Work here for an hour" shows "Here until 3:40". At the hour, one line: "Your hour is up. Stay, or go to LetsO bugs." No alarm, no jump. Off by default; hers to test and drop (time aids are unverified in adults with ADHD).
- **No project** is a room like the others, with one line: drag a row onto a project in the sidebar, or leave them. Rows drag from any room onto a sidebar project.
- **Focus** keeps a way back: "Back to Release 15.1.0" in the session head.

## Amended

D1: the sidebar's groups become the project list; Older stays. I3 and D5: "Rounds" now means the accordion inside a room.
