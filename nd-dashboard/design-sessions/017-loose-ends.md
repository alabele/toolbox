# 017 — Loose ends, not a fairy (rejected)

**Asked:** the parked "fairy": a tidy-up assistant that closes stale sessions, archives dead waits, merges duplicate projects.

**Outcome:** built 2026-10-16, removed the same day. She said: "I don't think I like or need this loose ends thing." The four-day session line and the Good place slot are gone. The five-day wait question, which she asked for separately, stays on the wait row.

**Roundtable:** interaction designer, lived-experience advocate. The advocate vetoed the idea as proposed; both agreed on the shape below.

## Decided

- **No helper, no name.** "Fairy" reads as a cute AI that knows better than she does. The thing is called Loose ends, and it has no voice of its own.
- **It asks, it never acts.** One plain line about one thing, two answers of equal weight, at a natural break. The only thing that runs on its own is Ledger summaries, which already do.
- **One at a time, never a count.** The stale-wait question ("6 days. Still worth waiting for?") lives in the Good place plate, only when nothing in the room needs her. Answering one does not bring up the next.
- **A your-turn session with no reply in four days** carries one line on its open plate: "No reply here in 4 days. It stays on disk if closed." with Close session and Leave it open. Leave it open means never ask about that one again.
- **Words it never uses:** stale, dead, neglected, abandoned, behind, "you haven't". No ages on her items elsewhere. No weekly summary.
- **Duplicate projects** wait: rare, and a merge touches keys and assignments. Later, behind undo.
- **Never closes a PR on GitHub.** That is public.

## Amended

The five-day wait question (built 2026-10-16) moved from the wait row into the Good place. The four-day threshold is a guess; the harness scorer checks it against real session ages.
