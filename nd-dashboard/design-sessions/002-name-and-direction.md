# Session 002: Name and direction

**Date**: 2026-09-22. **Decided by**: the user, from three candidates the design roundtable produced.

## Decision
The app is called Stillroom, the room in an old house where remedies were distilled and kept. The "witchy apothecary" direction is a feeling about keeping, labeling, and tending sessions, expressed through materials and ritual, not through illustration.

## Why
The user said "I don't think the name Quiet really works anymore" and asked for a witchy apothecary vibe. The lived-experience advocate read the wish as wanting a place that is hers: kept, labeled, tended. The designer proposed Stillroom (calm, a working space), Alembic (colder), and Tincture (names a product, not a place). She chose Stillroom.

## Constraints the roundtable attached
- No textures behind text, no candle flicker, no cute copy that grates by the fiftieth time.
- Amber and red only for real states, never as decoration.
- The ritual lives in how sessions are parked and named, not in ornament.

## Consequences
Park-with-a-note, Let it go, and click-to-rename exist because of this session.
