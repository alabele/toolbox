# 009 — Waiting on others, and her own order

**Asked:** "I just put up a PR, closed the Stillroom session, and am now waiting on others to review it. High priority, but I'm blocked. I don't want to lose it, and I don't want to hold it in my head." Also: "I need the next most urgent one to appear in Rounds, not just the next one," and "something in addition to pinned, like high/med/low priority."

**Roundtable:** executive-function coach, flow and priority specialist, interaction designer.

## Decided

- **The unit is the ticket; the PR stands in for it.** Closing the session is the hand-off. Nothing else to do. The close line says so: "Closed. docs-pipeline #319 is waiting on others. It comes back under Needs you when someone acts."
- **Waiting on others** is a sidebar group of pull requests in someone else's court: waiting on review, checks running, approved and in QA. One line says what it waits for and since when. Click a row and it opens on the Pull requests screen. It never leaves by age. Its only exits: into Needs you when someone acts, into the Ledger as merged, or the Shelf after three weeks.
- **Coming back.** Changes asked for, checks failing, merge conflict, or ready to merge moves the PR under Needs you in the sidebar and into Rounds as a plate, with "This is the one you were waiting on." The plate offers Open its session, Start a session on this, Open on GitHub.
- **Merged** by someone else lands in the Ledger as done: "docs-pipeline #319 merged by X."
- **Priority is hers, three words:** High, Med, Low, set in the session head or on the open Rounds plate. High shows as a small gold word on the row. Med shows nothing. Rounds opens High first, then Med, then Low. Inside a level, her Pinned order decides, then the oldest wait. PRs come after sessions.
- **Priority and blocked are separate.** A blocked item keeps its priority but sits in Waiting, never in the next slot. When it returns it lands by its priority.
- **No number that reads as behind.** Waiting shows a count on its heading like every group, but the badge on Rounds counts only what needs her.

## Amended

D1 allows six groups. Pinned stays hers; the machine never writes there.
