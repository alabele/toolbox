# 008 — Sidebar rows that tell apart

**Asked:** "Can this be more distinguished here?" Seven sidebar items, each the same plate: bold two-line title, the same "Your turn" mark and word on every row, a two-line preview starting "Done." with markdown showing through. Also: quick edit of titles in the sidebar.

**Roundtable:** interaction designer (structure, words), art director (look).

## Decided

- **The group is the plate. Rows are lines.** One outline per group, a hairline between rows, no gap. The open row is the only framed one, with the stronger cream outline from session 006.
- **No state mark on rows** in Your turn, Working and Older. The heading already says it. Pinned and Needs you mix states, so their rows keep a small mark and word at the right.
- **A repo monogram** at the left of each row, two letters in mono, tertiary ink, fixed width. Same repo, same letters, so rows cluster by eye. This amends D2: the repo is not the lead, but a quiet mark of it is allowed.
- **Titles on one line,** medium weight, ellipsis at the end. **Preview on one line:** her note if she left one, else the reply's first sentence with the status word and any markdown stripped.
- **Title rule changes.** Lead with the thing, then the matter: ticket, PR, file or repo first, then a noun phrase, no verb, three to six words. "VDC-2318 demo driver startup", not "Fixing demo page driver startup for VDC-2318". Every cached title gets rewritten once under the new rule. A title she typed sticks.
- **Gold once:** the Needs you heading, only when it has items. Other headings are tertiary ink. The star is cream, filled when on. The selected row shows by its frame, not by color.
- **Quick rename:** a pencil appears on hover at the right of the title. Click it, the title becomes a field with the text selected. Enter saves, Escape puts the old one back, clicking away saves. No double-click, since the first click would open the session.

## Tells removed

Same plate on every row. All-caps label on every row. Two-line clamps on title and preview both. Markdown fragments in previews. Gold spent three times.
