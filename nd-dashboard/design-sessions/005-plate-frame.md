# Session 005: The plate frame

**Date**: 2026-09-22. **Decided by**: the user, approving the art director's proposal after the critic's verdict.

## Decision
The rounded card with a colored left rail is gone. Its replacement is the plate: square corners, one thin outline, the label breaking the top edge, no fill, no rail. A gold outline means "needs you"; everything else gets a faint cream outline. The same frame carries the Next card, the "You asked" panel, and prompt cards. In Rounds, each item is a document with no container; the vine divides them, and a moon-phase mark in the margin shows position in the pass.

Gold is spent once per screen: the callout, and the one button that answers her. Eyebrows, Start, and corner marks go cream. Moons and vine stay, dimmer.

## Why
Three screens, one tell: every block was a rounded box with a colored rail, and gold sat on nearly everything. That is the generic card. The critic failed I3 (boldness spent on filled buttons) and T7 (mono on prose) for the same cause. The plate comes from her gold icon set, where each mark sits in a thin arched frame with its name on the edge.

## Rule changes
- D4 now allows radius 0 for plates. Pills stay round, controls stay 6.
- I2 clarified: mono is for code and paths only, never for hints or counts.
- Titles stay sentence case at light tracking; wide-tracked uppercase is for labels, pills, and buttons. Session 003 is amended by this note.
- The Stillroom light mode is removed; it was the cream-and-terracotta tell. Momentum's light themes remain.

## Rejected
- Keeping the rail as a thinner line: still a rail.
- Tinting plates by state: color alone, fails M2.

## Amendment, same day
The critic's second pass found the plate and the gold budget applied to the main surfaces only. Sidebar entries, the composer, the callout, and small controls now follow the same frame. I3 clarified: in Rounds, gold once per item.
