# 019 — The fairy

**Asked:** "I think I would still like a fairy feature of some kind." Then: both halves, tidy while away and set the table, as a button icon in the top right nav, never a pop-up; tidying runs on its own while she is away, setting the table is opt-in; the table is "a self navigation to-do list, something similar to the Slack triage".

**Decided by her,** over the roundtable's veto of the same day (session 017). Her call.

## Decided

- **A wand in the top bar**, beside the settings gear. A small dot when it did something she has not seen. Pressing it opens a panel like the settings menu. Nothing pops up on its own.
- **While you were out.** At midnight on weekdays, once a night (her amendment the same day; the first cut ran after an hour away): sessions on her turn and quiet for four days are closed, with a hand-off written first; waits she was asked about and left for five days are let go; loose sessions are filed into a project by key or words, or by one Haiku call that files only when sure. Each line in the panel has Undo, plus "Undo all of the last tidy". "Tidy now" runs it by hand.
- **The table.** A to-do list she walks herself: one row per thing, in her project order, each with a title that links to the thing, the next concrete step, and one reason it sits that high. Rows tick off and stay ticked through a re-set the same day. Set only when she presses "Set the table now" or opts in to "Set it when I arrive" (then it refreshes on open when older than six hours).
- **Words.** Lines read as plain facts: "Closed X, quiet for 5 days. Its hand-off is written." Never stale, dead, neglected, or "you haven't". No totals on the wand.

## Open

The four-day and five-day thresholds are guesses. The harness scorer checks them against real session ages at the November rerun.
