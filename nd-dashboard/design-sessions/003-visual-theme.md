# Session 003: Visual theme

**Date**: 2026-09-22. **Decided by**: the user, through four iterations and two references.

## Decision
Near-black with a hint of navy, cream text, antique gold for hairlines and anything that needs her. Periwinkle for working, moon-cream for your turn, rust for failed. Jost, wide-tracked and uppercase, for titles, labels, pills, and buttons. IBM Plex Sans for reading, IBM Plex Mono for code. Celestial line marks: moon phases in the bar, orbit and moon-star for states, a faint star field. No serif.

## References she supplied
- A Pinterest board: dark folk-botanical illustration, serpents and moon phases, moss greens, gold line art, engraved type.
- A gold celestial icon set on black: stars, moons, suns, planets, all antique gold line work.
- The Atelier of Witch Hat cover: navy, cream, rust, and a wide-tracked geometric sans for the title.

## Rejected, in order
1. Warm beige with a teal accent and a hyperlegible sans: "a grey list of boxes".
2. Momentum's Calm Waters navy: "a step in the right direction", kept as an option.
3. The cabinet: warm brown-purple, brass, Fraunces serif: "makes me nauseous".
4. The board's moss green and gold with Cormorant and Cinzel: green rejected, Cinzel rejected, serif rejected.

## What survived every round
Dark ground, one warm accent, no motion, celestial marks, and her own Momentum themes one click away.

## Consequences
Rules I1, I2, and M4 in DESIGN_GOVERNANCE.md. Any change to the palette or the type is a new session, not a fix.
