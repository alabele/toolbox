# Session 004: Two modes

**Date**: 2026-09-22. **Decided by**: the user, describing "a single project focus view and a power hour view".

## Decision
Two ways to work. Focus: one session, its latest exchange, earlier history behind a button. Rounds: every session that needs her as a card, oldest first, each with the pending prompt or the latest reply rendered in place and a reply box.

## Why
She asked for sidebar items that she could read and answer without switching, and named the two postures herself. The interaction model matches Cooper's sovereign and transient postures: Rounds and Focus are sovereign, the sidebar is transient.

## Rules that came from it
- Sidebar order is Needs you, Your turn, Working, Older; Working and Older start collapsed. "Needs you" is blocked work; "Your turn" is finished work.
- A session opens on its latest exchange only.
- Tool steps are hidden behind a switch.
- Every new session runs without permission prompts; she asks for a plan in the prompt when she wants one.
